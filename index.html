Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Animal Sounds & Names for Kids ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you and your children use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting children's privacy and complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Animal Sounds & Names for Kids is designed for children aged 0–10 and families. We do not ask for, collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, photos, or precise location data. The App does not require user registration or account creation.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

To keep the App free for families, we display child-appropriate advertisements provided by Google AdMob, a Google Play Families Self-Certified Ads SDK.

  • Child-Directed Treatment: The App configures Google AdMob with tagForChildDirectedTreatment and tagForUnderAgeOfConsent enabled, and restricts ad content strictly to General Audiences (G rating).
  • No Personalized Ads: Interest-based advertising, remarketing, and behavioral profiling are completely disabled.
  • Limited Technical Data: Google AdMob may automatically process limited, non-personal technical signals (such as device type, IP address for general country-level contextual serving, and app interaction events for ad frequency capping and fraud prevention) in strict compliance with Google's Privacy Policy and Families Policy requirements.

3. Parental Gate & Local Storage

The App includes an optional parent-gated feature allowing parents to temporarily disable ads for 30 minutes after watching a rewarded advertisement. The expiration timestamp for this ad-free session is stored strictly locally on your device and is never transmitted to any external server.

4. Offline Functionality & Permissions

All animal illustrations, voice pronunciations, and sound effects are bundled locally inside the App. The App only requires standard internet permission to load family-safe advertisements when a network connection is available.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect updates in legal or platform requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
